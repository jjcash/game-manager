## Accepted product decisions and remaining work

On **2026-11-08**, the user approved recording off by default with explicit opt-in; confirmed durable recording admission before native observation; a 10,000-record pause without automatic pruning; mandatory minimal explicit terminal-history deletion before production persistence ships; and restricted deletion of games, installations and LaunchProfiles while referenced history remains. The main cost is that unavailable persistence/full history skips observation, and users must deliberately remove history before deleting its referenced metadata.

This resolves the product package, not production verification. Dedicated export format, detached historical identities, configurable retention and attribution editing remain deferred. Concrete SQL, Rust types, provisional protocol constants and fault-seam mechanics require implementation validation against section 8. M2.1 feasibility is completed and merged within its tested scope; M2.2 remains unimplemented and separately authorized work. The original transcript and optional-suggestion details remain unavailable; the user-supplied A–H findings are reconciled above. The post-merge CI failure remains unresolved.
